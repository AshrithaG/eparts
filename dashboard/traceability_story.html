What this page is for. Our requirements, design decisions, meeting commitments and risks are one connected graph: 189 artifacts and 764 links. The reason that is worth the effort is rework. Twice now the cause was an early step, and fixing it there meant the three artifacts downstream did not have to be redone. The second board is the clearest case.

ADR-020: Pin one published release of the outside product-category standard (3 decisions depend on it · 1 risk, added July)

Board 2 · the gap this found. We depend on an outside industry standard for product categories, and we deliberately locked ourselves to one published version of it so the data stays consistent. When we wrote that decision up we stated the consequence out loud: if suppliers move to a newer version before we do, some products stop classifying and fall to manual review. Three later design decisions were built on that pin. The consequence was accepted in writing in April and did not reach the risk register until July, so for three months nobody owned it and nothing would have told us it had started happening. We found it by walking these links from the decision down towards risks and landing on nothing. It is tracked now, with an owner and a trigger, and ADR-020 itself says to revisit it before any production transition.
