## Requirement statement

**ML confidence scoring on every predicted attribute** — binds engineering + ML teams to measurable delivery for the eParts catalog program.

## Expanded description

### Behavior

- Emit per-attribute scalar or vector confidence after model forward pass.
- Feed scores into routing policy: auto-accept band, review band, reject band (thresholds ADR-governed).
- Log score distributions for calibration regression tests.
