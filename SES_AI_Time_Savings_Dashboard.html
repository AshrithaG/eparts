Methodology & Assumptions

How these measurements are derived and how they map to SES §6 metric categories.

Scope of This Dashboard

  • Only work the team has actually completed
  • Transcripts, requirements, SOW, risk, architecture diagrams, project plans
  • No coding, ingestion, OCR, ML, or pipeline work — those phases haven't started
  • Dashboard will be re-baselined as new phases complete

What "Saved" Means

  • Wall-clock hours of human effort removed per occurrence
  • Excludes AI tool runtime (treated as background cost)
  • Includes the human review/edit pass required by SES gates
  • Counts only artifacts that reached "Approved" or "Baselined" state

SES §6 Metrics Tracked

  • 6.3 AI Effectiveness: prompt count, re-prompt rate, correction volume, time saved vs. baseline
  • 6.4 Coordination: meeting time, review latency, documentation churn
  • 6.2 Process: cycle time per artifact, rework frequency

Caveats

  • Figures are team estimates against a manual-only baseline
  • Savings depend on review quality — high re-prompt rates erode the gain
  • No proprietary data is sent to external AI tools (per SES §5.2)
  • Numbers will shift as more transcripts/docs accumulate
